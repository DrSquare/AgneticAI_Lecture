# Physical Intelligence (π) — startup profile

*Window: 16 Oct 2025 – 16 Oct 2026 · Prepared 16 Oct 2026 · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*HQ: San Francisco · Founded 2024 · CEO: Karol Hausman · Approach: robot foundation models (software only, hardware-agnostic)*

## 1 Funding & valuation
- **Series B, $600M at $5.6B** — led by CapitalG; Lux, Thrive, Sequoia, Index, T. Rowe Price, OpenAI and Jeff Bezos participating. Total disclosed funding ~$1.1B. *(Nov 2025)* [1][2]
- **Reported:** talks to raise ~$1B at >$11B with Founders Fund and Lightspeed. **Close not confirmed.** *(28 Mar 2026 — Bloomberg, reported)* [2][3]

## 2 Technology
- **π\*0.6** trained with "Recap" (demonstrations + teleoperator corrections + autonomous practice); company says throughput roughly doubled on laundry folding and espresso making. *(18 Nov 2025)* [2]
- Human-video transfer emerges with scale, without special capture hardware *(17 Dec 2025)*; fine-tuned π0.6 took gold-tier results in 3 of 5 "Robot Olympics" categories *(22 Dec 2025)*. [2]
- **Multi-Scale Embodied Memory (MEM)** for tasks longer than ~10 minutes *(4 Mar 2026)*; **RL Tokens** for fast online RL on precise tasks such as screwdriving *(20 Mar 2026)*. [2]
- **π0.7** — compositional generalisation, combining skills on hardware it was not trained on. *(16 Apr 2026)* [2]
- Open-sourced π0 code (Feb 2025, ⚠ >12 months). [3]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **No product revenue disclosed**; one trade outlet described the company as pre-revenue (Mar 2026). [2]
- Published live-deployment data from partners **Weave Robotics** (laundry) and **Ultra** (industrial packaging). *(25 Feb 2026)* [2]
- **Ultra** raised a $50M Series A for warehouse robots and deepened its π partnership. *(9 Oct 2026)* [2]
- **API beta** opened for robot-model training and inference, with early manufacturing and heavy-machinery examples. *(14 Oct 2026)* — first visible monetisation path. [2]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- **Not applicable** — no own hardware; scale comes through partner robots (AgiBot research partnership Apr 2025, ⚠ >12 months). [2]
- Pilots include laundry folding in SF short-term rentals and box folding at a chocolate maker's back room. [1]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- Founding team from Google DeepMind, Stanford and UC Berkeley (Hausman, Levine, Finn, Ichter, Groom, Esmail, Vuong). [1]
- Key risk: valuation rests on model leadership, not paid deployments; competition from Skild AI (similar model layer, now with revenue) and from vertically integrated humanoid makers training their own models. [3]
- A buy-out rumour was reported and denied (date not given in source; treat as unverified). [3]
