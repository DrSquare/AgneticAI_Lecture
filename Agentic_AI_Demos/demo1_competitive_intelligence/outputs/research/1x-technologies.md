# 1X Technologies — startup profile

*Window: 16 Oct 2025 – 16 Oct 2026 · Prepared 16 Oct 2026 · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*HQ: Palo Alto, CA (factory: Hayward, CA; Moss, Norway) · Founded 2014 (as Halodi) · CEO: Bernt Børnich · Approach: consumer home humanoid (NEO) + video world model*

## 1 Funding & valuation
- **Reported:** SoftBank negotiating a **majority stake at ~$6B** — below the ≥$10B 1X sought in 2025. Not confirmed. *(Aug 2026 — reported)* [2]
- Last confirmed priced round: $100M Series B led by EQT Ventures at ~$820M *(Jan 2024, ⚠ >12 months)*; ~$125M disclosed in total. [1][2]

## 2 Technology
- **NEO** home humanoid (NEO Gamma design); on-board compute on NVIDIA Jetson Thor. [2]
- **1XWM world model** — video-trained "cognitive core" that predicts frames to generate actions *(20 Jan 2026)*; **1X World Model Lab** launched under Samarth Sinha *(4 Jun 2026)*. [2]
- 25-DoF tendon-driven hands with forearm actuators *(Jul 2026)*. [2]
- **Autonomy gap:** early units rely on remote human teleoperators for tasks policies cannot complete; CEO targets full autonomy in 2027. [1][2]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **NEO pre-orders** opened 28 Oct 2025 at **$20,000 or $499/month**; company says the initial 10,000-unit allocation booked in five days. [1][2]
- **EQT deal:** up to 10,000 NEO units offered to EQT's 300+ portfolio companies, 2026–2030, starting with US pilots *(Dec 2025)*. [2]
- **Deliveries: none confirmed in sources** — first US consumer shipments targeted for late 2026. CEO goal of 50,000 robots across home and enterprise *(Sep 2026)*. [2]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- **NEO Factory, Hayward CA** opened: 58,000 sq ft, vertically integrated, first-year capacity 10,000 units, 200+ staff *(29–30 Apr 2026)*. [2]
- Second Bay Area facility targeting 100,000 units/year by end-2027. [2]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- **Leadership churn:** VP of AI Eric Jang departed *(22 Jan 2026)*; co-founder Phuong Nguyen left *(4 Nov 2025)*. New CFO Bill Nash *(Jul 2026)*; VP Engineering Tom Sanocki *(Jun 2026)*. [2]
- Partners: NVIDIA (compute), EQT (investor and channel), OpenAI Startup Fund (early investor). [1][2]
- **Key risks:** privacy concerns over teleoperated home robots (WSJ, Oct 2025); valuation reset in reported SoftBank talks; delivery timeline slippage. Potential tailwind from proposed US limits on Chinese humanoid imports *(Jul 2026)*. [1][2]
