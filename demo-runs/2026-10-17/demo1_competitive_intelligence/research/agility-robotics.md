# Agility Robotics — startup profile
*Window: 2025-10-17 – 2026-10-17 · Prepared 2026-10-17 · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*Salem, Oregon · Founded 2015 (historical company baseline) · CEO Peggy Johnson · Integrated industrial humanoid hardware, physical AI, and fleet software.* [4][10]

Research cutoff: 2026-10-17, America/Los_Angeles. Publication dates appear at bullet ends; underlying measurement periods are identified separately.

## 1 Funding & valuation
- The June merger announcement sets a **$2.5B pre-money equity valuation** for the proposed Churchill Capital Corp XI combination. This is an agreed transaction mark, not a closed round or public market capitalization. *(2026-06-24)* [1]
- SEC effectiveness was announced October 13; the shareholder vote is **scheduled for November 2, 2026**. More than $620M expected gross proceeds include approximately $424M trust cash before redemptions and approximately $200M PIPE commitments. Closing remained conditional at this cutoff. *(2026-10-13)* [2]
- The S-4/A discloses **$100M raised through SAFEs in July 2026**. A complete comparable cumulative-capital figure was not verified in this run, so the matrix leaves it blank. Do not add expected SPAC proceeds to closed funding. *(2026-09-30)* [3]

## 2 Technology
- Digit 5 was unveiled September 15 with cooperative-safety architecture, NVIDIA IGX Thor/Halos integration, interchangeable grippers, and a stated 50 lb payload. These are company-described capabilities; general availability is planned by end-2027, with early access in H1 2027. *(2026-09-15)* [5]
- Nyobolt was named the battery technology provider. Agility states a planned 90-minute runtime/9-minute recharge cycle; this is a future-product specification, not independently measured fleet uptime. *(2026-10-15)* [6]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **FY2025 net sales: $1.782M**, corroborated in the definitive prospectus's indexed table in USD thousands. Full-file retrieval was blocked, so this check is limited to the accessible primary-source extract; it is historical revenue, not 2026 ARR. *(2026-10-13)* [4]
- Company-reported Digit 5 orders exceeded **$300M as of May 2026**: potential value across a three-year RaaS contract for 1,000 robots, conditional on milestones, with purchaser warrants vesting as deployment occurs. A SEC draft identifies concentration in one customer. These are not delivered robots or current-period sales. *(2026-10-06; draft indexed August 2026)* [7][11]
- Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada signed a commercial RaaS agreement after a pilot. Agility also names GXO and Schaeffler among customers; the investor deck reports nine committed facility deployments and 65,000 operating hours as of May 2026, unaudited company metrics. *(2026-02-19; 2026-10-06)* [8][7]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- RoboFab in Salem is designed for **up to 10,000 robots annually at full capacity**. Actual annual output is not disclosed in the reviewed sources; capacity must not be represented as deliveries. *(2026-09-15)* [5]
- A 60,000-square-foot Fremont development hub opened in July; nearly 200 hires were planned, not confirmed completed hires. The article's dateline is July 16 while the press index labels July 17; this discrepancy is retained. *(2026-07-16 dateline / 2026-07-17 index)* [9]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- Michael Beer became CFO and Jennifer Hunter moved to a dedicated COO role, supporting financing and manufacturing execution under CEO Peggy Johnson. *(2026-07-23)* [10]
- Investor probes: conditional order conversion/customer concentration; Digit 5 safety and manufacturing execution; financing continuity if the combination fails or suffers redemptions. The S-4/A includes substantial-doubt going-concern language even after the July SAFE financing. *(2026-09-30; 2026-10-06)* [3][7]
