# Skild AI — startup profile

*Window: 16 Oct 2025 – 16 Oct 2026 · Prepared 16 Oct 2026 · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*HQ: Pittsburgh (+ Bay Area, Bengaluru) · Founded 2023 · CEO: Deepak Pathak; President: Abhinav Gupta · Approach: "omni-bodied" robot brain licensed across robot types*

## 1 Funding & valuation
- **Series C, ~$1.4B at >$14B** — led by SoftBank Group, with NVentures, Macquarie Capital, Bezos Expeditions; Lightspeed, Felicis, Coatue and Sequoia doubled down; strategic investors Samsung, LG, Schneider Electric, CommonSpirit, Salesforce Ventures. Total funding >$2B. *(14 Jan 2026 — company press release)* [1][2]
- Prior: $300M Series A at $1.5B (Jul 2024, ⚠ >12 months); reported Series B at ~$4.5B (2025, size unconfirmed). [1][2]

## 2 Technology
- **Skild Brain**: one foundation model controlling quadrupeds, humanoids, tabletop arms and mobile manipulators without prior knowledge of the body; pre-trained on human video and physics simulation; adapts in context to lost limbs, jammed wheels or new payloads. [1]
- **S1** manipulation model: performs unseen tasks of up to ~10 minutes from a single video prompt with frozen weights; company reports 66% average step success vs 9% for a language-prompted baseline. No weights, API or paper released. *(25 Aug 2026)* [2]
- Simulated self-play (>140 simulated years) to learn soccer. *(23 Sep 2026)* [2]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **$100M ARR across 60+ enterprise customers**, ~10 months after first commercial deployment; named customers include **Foxconn, Mitsui & Co., Sumitomo Wiring Systems, G10 Fulfillment**. *(10 Sep 2026 — company-reported, unaudited)* [2]
- ~$30M revenue in 2025 and reportedly profitable (company-reported). [1][2]
- Deployment areas: inspection, last-mile delivery, warehouses, manufacturing, data centres, construction. [1]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- Asset-light: deploys on partner hardware. Announced work with **ABB Robotics and Universal Robots** to deploy Skild Brain *(Mar 2026, terms not disclosed)*. [2]
- **Acquired Zebra Technologies' robotics automation business (ex-Fetch Robotics)** for cash + equity, mainly for deployment talent. *(16 Apr 2026)* [2]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- Opened Bengaluru engineering office *(Feb 2026)*. [2]
- Publicly criticises robotics "demo culture" — positioning on paid deployments. [2]
- Risks: ARR and profitability are self-reported; S1 claims not peer-reviewed; dependence on hardware partners who may build or license competing models.
