# Figure AI — startup profile

*Window: 16 Oct 2025 – 16 Oct 2026 · Prepared 16 Oct 2026 · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*HQ: San Jose, CA · Founded 2022 · CEO: Brett Adcock · Approach: full-stack humanoid (hardware + Helix VLA model)*

## 1 Funding & valuation
- **Series C, >$1B at $39B post-money** — led by Parkway Venture Capital, with Brookfield, NVIDIA, Macquarie, Intel Capital, Qualcomm Ventures, Salesforce, T-Mobile Ventures and others. *(16 Sep 2025 — company announcement; ⚠ 1 month outside the 12-month window, kept as the latest priced round)* [1][2]
- **Total raised: ~$1.9B** (Sacra estimate, Jun 2026). [2]
- **No IPO or secondary pricing disclosed** as of Jul 2026. [2]
- Earlier: $675M at $2.6B (Feb 2024) from Bezos, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Intel, Amazon and OpenAI funds. *(⚠ >12 months old, context only)* [1]

## 2 Technology
- **Figure 03** launched 9 Oct 2025: cameras with 2× frame rate and ¼ latency, palm cameras, fingertip tactile sensing to ~3 g, inductive charging, washable soft exterior. *(⚠ just outside window)* [1][2]
- **Helix 02** (27 Jan 2026) extends the in-house vision-language-action model to full-body loco-manipulation; demos include dishwasher loading and laundry. [1][2]
- Perception-conditioned whole-body control for stairs/ramps (Apr 2026); two-robot coordination via a shared policy (May 2026). [2]
- **Project Go-Big** with Brookfield for egocentric human-video data collection (Sep 2025). [2]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **BMW Spartanburg (Figure 02):** company-disclosed 1,250+ runtime hours, 90,000+ sheet-metal parts loaded, contribution to 30,000+ X3 vehicles; Figure 02 retired to HQ after deployment. *(19 Nov 2025 — company disclosure, not independently audited)* [2]
- **Figure 03 at BMW Hall 52** for a more complex sequencing task. *(30 Jun 2026)* [2]
- **Catalyst Brands** — deployment at a Reno, NV distribution centre; first logistics customer outside automotive. *(26 May 2026)* [2]
- Week-long package-handling livestream; robot ran at ~98.5% of a human's throughput over a 10-hour head-to-head. *(May 2026)* [1]
- **Revenue: not disclosed.** [2]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- **BotQ** reached ~1 robot/hour (from 1/day in <120 days); **350+ Figure 03 units delivered**; battery-line first-pass yield 99.3%. *(29 Apr 2026 — company claim)* [2]
- First-generation BotQ capacity target: 12,000 robots/year (announced Mar 2025, ⚠ >12 months). [1][2]
- Estimated total commercial deployments still **<1,000 units** (mid-2026, secondary estimate). [2]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- Headcount ~650 (mid-2026). [2]
- **Lawsuit:** former head of product safety alleges wrongful termination after raising concerns about robot strength and injury risk. *(Nov 2025)* [1]
- OpenAI collaboration ended (2025); Figure now trains models in-house. [1]
- Earlier scrutiny of how BMW work was described (Fortune, Apr 2025, ⚠ >12 months). [1]
