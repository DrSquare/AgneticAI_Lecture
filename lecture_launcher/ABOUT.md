# Explore Agentic AI

Four saved workflows connect Define, Design and Build to practical outputs: robotics research, a fictional pitch panel, Alphabet valuation and NVIDIA annual-report analysis.

Choose a demo, explore its workflow nodes and follow the walkthrough. Files opens the reports and downloads; Sources explains dates, assumptions and definitions; Prompt helps you try the workflow yourself.

The timer, progress tracking, search, presentation view and dashboard controls work in your browser. The examples do not execute agents online. Open the workbooks in Excel to change assumptions and recalculate.

Press 1–4 to switch demos, P for presentation view and Escape to close a document. Print produces a clean version of the current view.

The examples are dated October 17, 2026. The pitch company and its data are fictional. Financial examples are teaching material with the relevant sources and limitations alongside their results.
