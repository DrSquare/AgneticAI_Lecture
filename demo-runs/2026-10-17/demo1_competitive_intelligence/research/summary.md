## Five differentiators

1. **Figure integrates the robot and proprietary AI.** BMW confirms a defined contribution to production and follow-on work. Paid fleet size and revenue remain unknown. [Figure profile](figure-ai.md)
2. **PI distributes models across partner robot platforms.** Its October 13 API beta expands access to training and inference. Partner performance statistics do not establish PI revenue. [PI profile](physical-intelligence.md)
3. **Skild has the clearest disclosed recurring-revenue signal in this sample.** September ARR and paying-customer claims remain unaudited here. Acquired robotics operations also make “software-only” too simple. [Skild profile](skild-ai.md)
4. **Agility combines focused logistics work with SPAC financial disclosure.** Conditional order conversion and funding continuity matter. A vote date does not establish a trading price. [Agility profile](agility-robotics.md)
5. **1X targets homes, where supervision and privacy are part of the product.** Capacity and a potential channel do not confirm external deliveries or autonomous household performance. [1X profile](1x-technologies.md)

## Momentum

These are analyst judgments from observable milestones, not measured market-share changes.

- **Gaining on milestones — Figure:** customer-confirmed follow-on work and technical releases. Revenue growth remains unknown.
- **Gaining in distribution — PI:** partner work and API beta. Paid conversion remains undisclosed.
- **Gaining on company-reported adoption — Skild:** commercial metrics and acquired operations. January “revenue” is not a comparable ARR starting point.
- **Under pressure from financing conditions — Agility:** pending SPAC and concentrated conditional orders. This does not establish declining customer demand.
- **Holding pending delivery evidence — 1X:** product and manufacturing milestones are observable. The evidence does not establish a worsening business or a confirmed down round.

## Three IC implications

1. **Commercial evidence should drive underwriting.** Request contracts, collections and retention. Reconcile ARR to recognized revenue and distinguish pilots, preorders and conditional orders. Public measures do not support a like-for-like revenue multiple across all five.
2. **Financing comparisons need a common basis.** Keep historical priced rounds, proposed SPAC values and reported talks separate. Reassess Agility after the vote and any closing without assuming either outcome. Obtain a verified capitalization table before using valuation / cumulative capital.
3. **Autonomy economics deserve dedicated diligence.** Measure interventions, productive utilization and service costs at customer sites. Capacity and model demonstrations cannot establish delivered-unit economics.

## Caveats

- Nondisclosure is not zero revenue or evidence that no later financing occurred.
- Agility's full definitive SEC document exceeded retrieval limits and its direct download failed. The FY2025 figure is corroborated by indexed primary table text, not a full-file audit.
- No robot, API, physical delivery, legal docket or native Office UI test was performed.
- The workbook stores numeric endpoints with qualifiers. Capital remains blank. Its valuation / capital formulas return “Not comparable” until exact matching inputs are available. Evidence-age formulas calculate from the fixed cutoff.
- No recurring automation is active. A partner should review the evidence before external IC circulation.
