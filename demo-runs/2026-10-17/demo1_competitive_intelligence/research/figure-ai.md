# Figure AI — startup profile
*Window: 2025-10-17 – 2026-10-17 · Prepared 2026-10-17 (America/Los_Angeles) · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*San Jose, California · Founded 2022 ⚠ historical identity baseline · CEO Brett Adcock · Approach: vertically integrated humanoid hardware, proprietary Helix models, training data and manufacturing.* [2][9][11]

## 1 Funding & valuation
- ⚠ Latest verified priced-round baseline: Figure announced more than $1 billion of **committed** Series C capital at a $39 billion post-money valuation, led by Parkway Venture Capital. The announcement predates this research window; it does not disclose an exact final amount or a complete cumulative capital total. *(2025-09-16)* [1]
- Nscale announced a strategic investment in Figure, without disclosing equity amount or valuation. Its separate $3.5 billion initial **compute commitment**, potentially exceeding $6 billion, is not a Figure fundraising amount. Total capital raised and latest investment amount: not disclosed in the verified primary sources. *(2026-09-03)* [2]
- Figure's shareholder notice says stock transfers require board approval and warns about unauthorized secondary transactions. No verified Figure AI IPO or SPAC transaction was found in the reviewed company sources. Secondary asking prices should not replace the dated financing valuation. *(2026-07-08; review through 2026-10-17)* [3]

## 2 Technology
- ⚠ Figure 03 was introduced just before the window as the latest named hardware generation verified here, with palm cameras, tactile hands, soft coverings and wireless charging. Treat its home-market design as product positioning, not proof of broad consumer deployment. *(2025-10-09)* [10]
- Helix 02 introduced full-body control, including a company-demonstrated four-minute kitchen task. These are company demonstrations, not independent safety or commercial reliability tests. *(2026-01-27)* [4]
- Helix 2.5 extended the model roadmap: Figure reports three behaviors evaluated in 30 previously unseen homes, with 56% complete-task success for Index-pretrained policies versus 9% without pretraining. “Zero-shot” concerns the evaluation homes and objects; task-specific adaptation occurred elsewhere. These are company-reported benchmarks, not a claim of arbitrary household competence. No open model release was identified in the reviewed releases. *(2026-09-17)* [5]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **Revenue: not disclosed.** No verified revenue, ARR, backlog value or public price was available in the reviewed primary announcements. BMW confirms that Figure 02 supported more than 30,000 BMW X3 vehicles over ten months in 2025 by placing sheet-metal parts; this does not mean robots assembled entire cars. *(2026-06-25; historical 2025 operating period)* [6]
- BMW announced a Figure 03 sequencing project in Spartanburg, a follow-on logistics application rather than a quantified fleet-wide rollout. This provides customer-side confirmation of continuing engagement. *(2026-06-25)* [6]
- Figure announced a commercial agreement with Catalyst Brands, beginning at its Reno distribution logistics center. Contract value, paid unit count and production service levels were not disclosed. *(2026-05-26)* [7]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- Figure reports more than 350 Figure 03 robots delivered from BotQ and a demonstrated one-robot-per-hour cycle time. The release allocates robots across internal R&D, data collection and commercial use-case development; it does not establish 350 external customer deliveries or annual sustained output. *(2026-04-29)* [8]
- ⚠ The latest verified explicit capacity plan in this review is up to 12,000 robots per year for the first BotQ line and a goal of 100,000 over four years. Both are company capacity/production targets, not achieved deliveries. *(2025-10-09)* [10]
- Figure states that most of the Figure 02 fleet has been decommissioned while Figure 03 expands. Third-party stories of a July 2026 1,000th Figure 03 were found, but the originating primary post was not retrieved; this profile therefore retains the dated April primary-source production baseline instead of treating 1,000 as verified. *(2026-09-30; research limitation assessed 2026-10-17)* [9]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- Brett Adcock remains identified as founder and CEO in the Nscale announcement. The partnership targets first GPU deployments in the second half of 2027 and up to 100,000 GPUs; these are prospective infrastructure plans. *(2026-09-03)* [2]
- Investor diligence inference: test capital intensity and financing coverage for the compute commitment; the undisclosed equity consideration prevents a reliable total-capital comparison. *(2026-09-03; analysis as of 2026-10-17)* [2]
- Investor diligence inference: require independent uptime, intervention, safety and unit-economics evidence before extrapolating home benchmark performance or a factory cycle time into profitable general deployment. Production maturity and robust autonomy remain separate questions. *(2026-09-17 and 2026-04-29; analysis as of 2026-10-17)* [5][8]
- Momentum assessment: **gaining**, based on the customer-confirmed BMW follow-on, Catalyst agreement and newer Helix research. This is an analyst judgment about evidence progression, not a claim about revenue growth. *(2026-06-25, 2026-05-26 and 2026-09-17; analysis as of 2026-10-17)* [6][7][5]
