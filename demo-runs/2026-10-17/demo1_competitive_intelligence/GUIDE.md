## Sources and methods

Research window: October 17, 2025 to October 17, 2026. Older financing benchmarks are dated explicitly.

Profiles link to company announcements, counterparty reports, filings and attributed press reports.

Company-reported operating metrics are unaudited. Proposed SPAC values and reported acquisition talks are not closed financing rounds.

## Reusable prompt

Research Figure AI, Physical Intelligence, Skild AI, Agility Robotics and 1X Technologies using a separate researcher for each. Batch researchers if the concurrency limit requires it. Use one explicit 12-month window and cutoff date. Use these exact headings: 1 Funding & valuation; 2 Technology; 3 Commercial traction; 4 Manufacturing & scale; 5 Team, partners & risks. Date every factual bullet and end it with a numbered source citation. Prefer filings, companies and counterparties. Label unaudited operating metrics company-reported, unconfirmed transactions reported, and older latest benchmarks with a warning. Do not equate capacity, production, orders and deliveries.

Save five profiles and structured evidence. Verify at least three material facts per company. Synthesize a side-by-side comparison, five differentiators, evidence-based momentum judgments and three IC implications. Build a formula-capable workbook with numeric inputs, visible sources, guarded valuation/capital ratios and useful evidence-age calculations. Produce and visually verify exactly three IC slides. Preserve source material. Do not activate automation, publish or send communications.
