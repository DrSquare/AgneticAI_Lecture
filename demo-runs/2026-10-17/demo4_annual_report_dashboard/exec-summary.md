# NVIDIA FY2026 | Board executive summary

Historical year ended January 25, 2026 [p.51 / PDF139](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=139). Prepared October 17, 2026. Outlook below was issued February 25, 2026 [Q4 release, Outlook](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx); it is not current guidance. Comparisons are versus the prior fiscal year. Amounts: USD million. pp: percentage points. calc: derived.

## 5 headlines

1. **Scale outpaced operating profit.** Revenue reached 215,938, up 65.47%; operating income grew 60.08%. Net margin slipped 0.25 pp to 55.60% (growth rates and margins: calc). [p.51 / PDF139](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=139)

2. **Data Center dominates the platform mix.** Its revenue share reached 89.72% (calc). Data Center is an end market, distinct from the Compute & Networking reportable segment; these views cannot be added. [pp.51,79 / PDF139,167](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=167)

3. **Investment marks complicate earnings repeatability.** Net income rose 64.75% (calc). [p.51 / PDF139](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=139) Management attributes higher other income primarily to unrealized equity gains. Analyst interpretation: operating profit merits attention alongside net income. [p.42 / PDF130](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=130)

4. **FCF excludes major investments.** Operating cash flow was 102,718; company-defined FCF was 96,575 (calc). FCF/net income fell 2.89 pp to 80.43% (calc). [pp.51,55 / PDF139,143](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=143) FCF excludes private-equity purchases and the Groq payment. [Q4 release, Non-GAAP Measures](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

5. **Historical outlook assumed continued growth.** Q1 FY2027 revenue guidance was 78,000 ±2%, assuming zero China Data Center compute revenue. This was an expectation, not an actual result. [Q4 release, Outlook](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

## 3 risks

1. **Demand concentration meets supply exposure.** Inventory rose 112.33% (calc) to 21,403; supply commitments were approximately 95,200. [pp.53,70 / PDF141,158](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=158) Anonymous direct customers represented 22% and 14% of revenue; identity continuity is unknown. [p.78 / PDF166](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=166) Analyst interpretation: slower concentrated demand could expose inventory and capacity.

2. **Partner exposures may move together.** Analyst interpretation: customer, investment and capacity relationships could amplify a financing slowdown. Counterparty overlap and funded demand are undisclosed; the evidence does not establish circular revenue. [pp.37,69,70 / PDF125,157,158](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=125)

3. **Margins and comparison bases need discipline.** GAAP gross margin fell 3.90 pp to 71.1%; net inventory-provision impact worsened 0.30 pp (changes: calc). Management cites the Blackwell transition and H20; a full causal bridge, exact annual net H20 effect and isolated Blackwell impact are undisclosed. [p.41 / PDF129](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2026/ar/2026-Annual-Report-Web.pdf#page=129) FY2027 non-GAAP includes stock-based compensation. [Q4 release, RECONCILIATION OF GAAP TO NON-GAAP OUTLOOK](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

## 3 questions

1. What demand-downside scenario, cancellation rights and reserve assumptions support supply commitments and inventory?

2. How much counterparty exposure overlaps across sales, investments and guarantees, and what liquidity remains after contractual protections?

3. Can management quantify the margin bridge, including net H20 effects, and provide comparable non-GAAP results including stock-based compensation, separately identifying export effects?
