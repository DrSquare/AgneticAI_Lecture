## Ranked narrative fixes and founder questions

1. **Rebuild the proof slide around paying activity.** Show paid versus unpaid customers and robots, the ARR definition and date, a test-population label for success, and cohort size for NRR (p. 6). Question: which signed and paying contracts support each metric and the Fortune 500 claim?
2. **Reconcile present economics before showing ambition.** Clearly mark actual versus target margin, CAC method/interval, hardware capital and remote-operations costs (p. 7). Question: what is acquisition-cohort payback after gross additions, churn, sales-cycle lag and all attributable costs?
3. **Connect the ask to a financed execution plan.** Tie paying robots and deployment capacity to hiring, cash and hardware (pp. 10-11). Question: which milestones are funded under base and downside plans, and what drives the 24-month runway?
4. **Show measured product advantage, with causal limits.** Report controlled or fixed-site/SKU results alongside operating boundaries and operator dependence (pp. 1, 3, 6, 8). Question: what evidence separates model improvement from customer/SKU mix changes, and what rights protect the data and model advantage?
5. **Make the initial segment and obtainable market specific.** Replace broad claims with customer evidence and reachable deployment assumptions (pp. 2, 5, 8). Question: how many eligible sites can the current product serve, against which alternatives, and at what total cost?

A short follow-up meeting is narratively supportable on corrected paying traction; a financing recommendation remains conditional on the cross-panel evidence checks (pp. 6-7, 10-11). Missing records are founder questions, not findings of falsity (pp. 2-5, 7-11).
