# Agility Robotics — startup profile

*Window: 16 Oct 2025 – 16 Oct 2026 · Prepared 16 Oct 2026 · Brief: startup-profile v1*
*HQ: Salem, OR · Founded 2015 · CEO: Peggy Johnson · Approach: purpose-built logistics humanoid (Digit), sold as Robots-as-a-Service*

## 1 Funding & valuation
- **Going public via SPAC** with Churchill Capital Corp XI at **$2.5B pre-money**; >$620M gross proceeds (~$424M trust, assuming no redemptions, + ~$200M PIPE led by **Foxconn**); ticker **AGLT** on Nasdaq. *(24 Jun 2026 — announcement)* [1][2]
- **SEC declared the S-4 effective**; Churchill XI shareholder vote set for **2 Nov 2026**, close expected Nov 2026. *(14 Oct 2026)* [3]
- >$390M equity raised since founding (investor presentation, Jun 2026). [1]

## 2 Technology
- **Digit 5** unveiled: 50-lb payload, swappable end-effectors, ~9-minute recharge (Nyobolt batteries), new safety architecture for fence-free work near people. Early access 2027; GA end-2027/early-2028. *(15 Sep 2026)* [2][3]
- First humanoid developer on **NVIDIA Halos** safety architecture *(Jun 2026)*; FORT offboard safety bridge *(1 Oct 2026)*. [2]
- Passed an OSHA-recognised NRTL field inspection at a live e-commerce site (ANSI/RIA R15.08, ISO 13849, ISO 12100). *(Nov 2025)* [2]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **S-4 financials: 2025 sales $1.78M; net loss $138.1M; going-concern warning.** *(7 Sep 2026 — SEC filing)* [2]
- **>$300M committed multi-year orders** for 1,000 Digit 5 units — a **single related-party customer**, milestone-based, partly paid in warrants; not current revenue. [1][2]
- Named customers: **Schaeffler, GXO, Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada** (RaaS after pilot, Feb 2026), **Mercado Libre** (San Antonio). 65,000+ real-world operating hours. [1][2][3]
- Pipeline: >30 potential customers, target ~100 by end-2027. [3]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- **RoboFab** (Salem, OR) designed for up to 10,000 robots/year; new Fremont, CA site for hardware and physical-AI development *(Jul 2026)*. [2][3]
- Digit 5 BOM ~$150K; RaaS margins near 50% until volume reaches ~1,000 units/year *(investor day, 6 Oct 2026)*. [2]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- New CFO Michael Beer; Jennifer Hunter moves to COO *(Jul 2026)*. Rebranded to "Agility" *(Mar 2026)*. [2]
- Amazon is an early investor (2022) with past pilots; active-customer status not confirmed. [1]
- **Key risks:** tiny current revenue vs. valuation, customer concentration in the backlog, going-concern language, SPAC redemption risk.
