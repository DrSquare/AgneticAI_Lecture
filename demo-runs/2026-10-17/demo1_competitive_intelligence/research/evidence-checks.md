# Material evidence checks

Prepared 2026-10-17 (America/Los_Angeles). Window 2025-10-17 through 2026-10-17.

Each independent researcher checked at least three material claims. Verified means the claim appears in the identified source, not that unaudited company metrics are audited. Agility financial-table evidence is indexed primary text because full-file retrieval failed.

## Figure AI

- **Claim:** Latest verified priced funding baseline is >$1B committed Series C at $39B post-money.
  - Date: 2025-09-16. [Figure Exceeds $1B in Series C Funding at $39B Post-Money Valuation](https://www.figure.ai/news/series-c).
  - Verdict: Verified as company announcement; outside research window.
  - Evidence: Announcement states capital commitments over $1B, $39B post-money, and Parkway lead. It does not state an exact final total.
- **Claim:** Figure 02 supported production of more than 30,000 BMW X3 vehicles in 2025.
  - Date: 2026-06-25. [BMW Group advances the use of Physical AI in production with Figure 03 project in Spartanburg](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/canada/article/detail/T0458794EN/bmw-group-advances-the-use-of-physical-ai-in-production-with-figure-03-project-in-spartanburg?showMedia=photo).
  - Verdict: Customer-confirmed; limited task contribution, not complete-car assembly.
  - Evidence: BMW describes ten months of sheet-metal positioning and a subsequent Figure 03 logistics project.
- **Claim:** Figure reported >350 Figure 03 from BotQ and demonstrated a one/hour cycle time.
  - Date: 2026-04-29. [Ramping Figure 03 Production](https://www.figure.ai/news/ramping-figure-03-production).
  - Verdict: Verified as company-reported manufacturing metric; external customer delivery count unverified.
  - Evidence: Release gives both figures and explains allocations to research, data collection and commercial development.
