# Skild AI — startup profile

*Window: 2025-10-17 – 2026-10-17 · Prepared 2026-10-17 · Brief: startup-profile v1*  
*HQ: Pittsburgh (investor record, undated, accessed 2026-10-17) [11] · Founded 2023 · CEO: Deepak Pathak; President: Abhinav Gupta (2026-09-29) [8] · Approach: cross-embodiment robot intelligence, now with acquired robotics operations (2026-04-15) [7]*

## 1 Funding & valuation
- **$1.4B Series C at over $14B valuation**, announced as raised; SoftBank led, with NVentures, Macquarie Capital and Bezos Expeditions participating. The valuation is a lower-bound statement, not an exact $14B post-money figure. *(2026-01-14)* [1]
- **Total raised: not verified in reviewed primary announcements.** Secondary reporting places cumulative funding above $2B. The numeric matrix therefore leaves total capital blank; no valuation/capital multiple is calculated. No IPO or SPAC transaction was verified in this research. *(2026-10-17 review; funding report 2026-01-14)* [1][10]

## 2 Technology
- **S1**, the flagship Skild Brain model, uses a video demonstration as context without updating model weights. Company experiments report **66% versus 9%** on unseen-task cumulative step success, with human recovery interventions; this is not an independently replicated end-to-end completion rate. *(2026-08-18, date from official index)* [3][4]
- **Physical Self-Play** presents robot soccer learning and sim-to-real transfer after **140 simulated years**, a company research result rather than evidence of reliable industrial deployment. Public model weights or a production API were not verified in the reviewed releases. *(2026-09-23)* [5]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **Company-reported, unaudited:** more than **$100M ARR**, **$50M recognized revenue** since first deployment ten months earlier, and **60+ paying customers**. These are different metrics; neither ARR nor recognized revenue should be treated as audited annual sales. *(2026-09-10)* [2]
- Skild describes deployment work with NVIDIA/Foxconn, planned manufacturing deployment with Sumitomo Wiring Systems, and a Mitsui kitchen pilot. These relationship stages do not establish that every named partner is a paying customer. *(2026-09-10)* [2]
- **Pittsburgh International Airport independently describes a proof-of-concept:** up to four robots are planned for air-quality and terminal operations testing. This is a pilot plan, not a verified four-unit delivery or paid contract. Pricing, backlog and contract economics were not disclosed in this source. *(2026-09-29)* [8]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- **Not applicable to the core model as a unit-production metric**, but “software-only” is now incomplete: Skild acquired Zebra's Robotics Automation business, formerly Fetch, with cash consideration and equity issued to Zebra. Deal value, Skild factory capacity and units delivered were not disclosed. *(2026-04-15)* [7]
- Scale strategy combines OEM distribution and robotics operations: announced partnerships cover ABB Robotics, Universal Robots and MiR; NVIDIA/Foxconn assembly integration was described prospectively in March. Do not count partner installed bases as Skild deployments. *(2026-03-19)* [6]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- The company opened its Bengaluru office, extending teams in California and Pennsylvania. The airport's September announcement identifies Pathak as CEO and Gupta as president; no leadership change is asserted here. *(2026-02-19; leadership confirmed 2026-09-29)* [9][8]
- **Analyst assessment — gaining:** financing, S1 and new deployment channels support momentum. Probe repeatable task reliability and intervention costs; validate ARR definitions, renewal rates and service margins; assess integration burden following the Zebra acquisition. These are diligence questions, not allegations. *(2026-10-17 assessment)* [1][2][3][7]
