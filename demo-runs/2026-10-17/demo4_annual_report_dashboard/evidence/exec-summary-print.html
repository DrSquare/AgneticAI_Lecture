5 headlines

  • Scale outpaced operating profit. Revenue reached 215,938, up 65.47%; operating income grew 60.08%. Net margin slipped 0.25 pp to 55.60% (growth rates and margins: calc). p.51 / PDF139
  • Data Center dominates the platform mix. Its revenue share reached 89.72% (calc). Data Center is an end market, distinct from the Compute & Networking reportable segment; these views cannot be added. pp.51,79 / PDF139,167
  • Investment marks complicate earnings repeatability. Net income rose 64.75% (calc). p.51 / PDF139 Management attributes higher other income primarily to unrealized equity gains. Analyst interpretation: operating profit merits attention alongside net income. p.42 / PDF130
  • FCF excludes major investments. Operating cash flow was 102,718; company-defined FCF was 96,575 (calc). FCF/net income fell 2.89 pp to 80.43% (calc). pp.51,55 / PDF139,143 FCF excludes private-equity purchases and the Groq payment. Q4 release, Non-GAAP Measures
  • Historical outlook assumed continued growth. Q1 FY2027 revenue guidance was 78,000 ±2%, assuming zero China Data Center compute revenue. This was an expectation, not an actual result. Q4 release, Outlook

3 risks

  • Demand concentration meets supply exposure. Inventory rose 112.33% (calc) to 21,403; supply commitments were approximately 95,200. pp.53,70 / PDF141,158 Anonymous direct customers represented 22% and 14% of revenue; identity continuity is unknown. p.78 / PDF166 Analyst interpretation: slower concentrated demand could expose inventory and capacity.
  • Partner exposures may move together. Analyst interpretation: customer, investment and capacity relationships could amplify a financing slowdown. Counterparty overlap and funded demand are undisclosed; the evidence does not establish circular revenue. pp.37,69,70 / PDF125,157,158
  • Margins and comparison bases need discipline. GAAP gross margin fell 3.90 pp to 71.1%; net inventory-provision impact worsened 0.30 pp (changes: calc). Management cites the Blackwell transition and H20; a full causal bridge, exact annual net H20 effect and isolated Blackwell impact are undisclosed. p.41 / PDF129 FY2027 non-GAAP includes stock-based compensation. 3 questions

  • What demand-downside scenario, cancellation rights and reserve assumptions support supply commitments and inventory?
  • How much counterparty exposure overlaps across sales, investments and guarantees, and what liquidity remains after contractual protections?
  • Can management quantify the margin bridge, including net H20 effects, and provide comparable non-GAAP results including stock-based compensation, separately identifying export effects?
