## C. Anomalies and watch items

| # | Anomaly | Evidence | Stated vs. interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Revenue +65% while gross margin fell 3.9 pts** | H20 charge $4.5B = 2.1% of revenue (calc: 4,500 ÷ 215,938); inventory provisions $7.2B vs $3.7B; net GM impact 2.6 pts vs 2.3 pts (10-K MD&A) | *Stated:* shift from Hopper HGX to Blackwell full-scale datacenter solutions, plus the H20 charge. *Interpretation:* about half the decline is the one-off H20 charge; the rest is product-transition mix. |
| A2 | **Net income grew faster than operating income** | NI +65% vs op. income +60%; other income 11,063 vs 2,573 = 7.8% of pre-tax income vs 3.1% (calc) | *Stated:* mainly unrealised gains on non-marketable and public equity, including Intel shares. *Interpretation:* ~$9B of mark-to-market gains is not recurring operating profit. |
| A3 | **Customer concentration jumped** | Largest direct customer 22% (FY25: 12%); top two = 36% of revenue (10-K MD&A) | *Stated:* both mainly Compute & Networking. *Interpretation:* revenue depends more on a few hyperscalers/integrators than a year ago. |
| A4 | **Inventory more than doubled** | Inventory 21,403 vs 10,080 (+112%) vs cost of revenue +91%; days of inventory 125 vs 113 (PR-Q4) | *Interpretation (calc: inventory ÷ cost of revenue × 365):* build-ahead for Blackwell/Rubin ramps; watch for further provisions. |
| A5 | **NVIDIA is financing parts of its own ecosystem** | $17.5B invested in private companies and infrastructure funds (= 18% of FCF, calc) plus $3.5B of land, power and shell guarantees for early-stage companies; investing outflow 52,228 vs 20,421 (10-K MD&A) | *Interpretation:* some investees are likely customers; a question for the Board on credit and circularity risk. |
| A6 | **International mix fell sharply** | Revenue from customers HQ'd outside the U.S. 31% vs 41% (10-K MD&A) | *Stated:* China export licensing (H20 licence required Apr 2025; ~$60M H20 revenue after Aug 2025 licences). *Interpretation:* China is effectively zero in DC compute guidance. |
| A7 | **Tax rate rising** | Effective rate 15.1% vs 13.3%; FY27 guide 17–19% (10-K MD&A, PR-Q4) | *Stated:* OBBBA effects; $711M state valuation-allowance release. |

## D. Restatements and one-offs
- **One-offs:** H20 charge $4.5B (Q1) with a $180M release in Q2 (PR-Q4); $711M tax valuation-allowance release (10-K MD&A); unrealised equity gains in other income (10-K MD&A).
- **Restatements:** none identified in the extract.
- **Definition change:** from Q1 FY27, non-GAAP measures include stock-based compensation (PR-Q4) — FY27 non-GAAP figures are not comparable to FY26 without adjustment.
