# 1X Technologies — startup profile

## 2 Technology
- NEO's consumer preorder launch introduced a soft-covered, tendon-actuated home humanoid; initial autonomous capabilities are bounded and unfamiliar chores can involve scheduled human experts. This is a company description, not an independent household performance benchmark. *(2025-10-28)* [3]
- **1XWM** derives actions from text-conditioned generated video through an inverse dynamics model. The company describes a 14B video backbone, 900 hours of egocentric human mid-training, 70 hours of robot fine-tuning, and a separate inverse dynamics model trained on 400 hours of robot data. These are company-reported research inputs, not deployment reliability. *(2026-01-12)* [4]
- Updated NEO hands have **25 DoF including the wrist** (22 in fingers/palm plus 3 wrist), according to 1X; avoid mixing this with the 22-DoF launch description. Capability and durability claims remain company-reported. *(2026-07-09)* [5]

## 3 Commercial traction
- **Revenue: not disclosed.** NEO preorder pricing was $20,000 for Early Access and $499/month for a later subscription; the live order page still shows a **$200 refundable deposit** and US deliveries starting in 2026. Neither the deposit nor listed price proves recognized revenue or completed shipment. *(2025-10-28 launch; order page accessed 2026-10-17)* [3][6]
- 1X says its first-year capacity of 10,000 NEOs was booked within five days of the preorder launch. Treat this as **company-reported preorder demand**, not 10,000 units delivered or $200 million of revenue. *(2026-04-30)* [7]
- The EQT partnership expresses an intent to facilitate **up to 10,000 potential deployments during 2026–2030**, with each portfolio company deciding whether to implement. It is not a firm purchase order or a named fleet of paying customers. IFS announced a joint industrial-development partnership, with commercial availability planned for 2026; realized customer revenue was not provided. *(2025-12-11 EQT; 2025-11-13 IFS)* [8][9]
- **Customer-home deliveries: not independently verified in the reviewed sources.** April production was directed to R&D and internal testing; the undated company timeline describes 2026 Early Access home deployment while the live order page retains forward-looking delivery language. These sources do not establish a delivered external customer count; absence of verification does not prove zero deliveries. *(2026-04-30 production update; live pages accessed 2026-10-17)* [6][7][10]

## 4 Manufacturing & scale
- Hayward's factory is company-reported as operational, with **58,000 sq ft and 200+ staff**. Its April update both describes capacity up to **10,000 NEOs per year** and says the operation is ramping toward that level. This is capacity, not measured annual production; **100,000+ per year by end-2027** is a target. *(2026-04-30)* [7]
- July's hand update says hundreds of hands had been manufactured and describes capacity for **10,000 hands in 2026**. That component count is not 10,000 complete robots and should not be silently combined with the factory's robot-capacity claim. *(2026-07-09)* [5]

## 5 Team, partners & risks
- VP of AI **Eric Jang announced his departure** in a first-person post. 1X subsequently announced **Bill Nash as CFO**. A departure is a documented leadership change; its effect on execution is an analyst question, not a measured setback. *(2026-01-21; 2026-07-02)* [12][13]
- The factory release names NVIDIA Jetson Thor and Isaac as part of the technology stack. IFS provides a path toward industrial integration, while EQT is an investor-linked access channel rather than a guaranteed buyer. *(2026-04-30; 2025-11-13; 2025-12-11)* [14][9][8]
- **Investor probes (analysis):** obtain verified customer shipments and sustained autonomous-task data; measure expert-operator cost and privacy controls; reconcile factory throughput, component capacity and financing runway. The world-model paper itself notes grounding errors, latency and remaining longer-horizon work. *(analysis as of 2026-10-17, based on dated and live sources)* [4][5][6][7]
- **Momentum: holding (analyst judgment).** Manufacturing and research advances are tangible announcements, but publicly verified home deployments and closed current financing remain unresolved. The label expresses evidence quality, not an investment recommendation. *(analysis as of 2026-10-17)* [1][4][5][7]
