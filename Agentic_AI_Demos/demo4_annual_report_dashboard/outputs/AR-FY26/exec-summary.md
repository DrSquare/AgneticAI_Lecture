# NVIDIA FY2026 — one-page executive summary for the Board (Stage 3 of 4)

*Reads only: `trends.md`. Every number keeps its source; "calc" = calculated in Stage 2.*

## Five headlines
1. **A record year: revenue $215.9B (+65%) and net income $120.1B (+65%).** Data Center was $193.7B, ~90% of revenue (10-K MD&A; PR-Q4; calc).
2. **Networking is the new growth engine:** Data Center networking +142% vs compute +59% (10-K MD&A).
3. **Margins dipped, then recovered:** full-year GAAP gross margin 71.1% (−3.9 pts), but quarterly margin climbed from 60.5% in Q1 to 75.0% in Q4 (10-K MD&A; PR-Q1–Q4).
4. **The year ended ahead of plan:** Q4 revenue $68.1B was 4.8% above the $65.0B guide; Q1 FY27 is guided to $78.0B (+14.5% QoQ, calc) with no China Data Center compute (PR-Q3; PR-Q4).
5. **Cash machine:** free cash flow $96.6B (+59%); $41.1B returned to shareholders, ~43% of FCF (PR-Q4; calc).

## Three risks
1. **Customer concentration** — two direct customers are 36% of revenue (22% + 14%), up from a top customer at 12% a year ago (10-K MD&A).
2. **Ecosystem financing** — $17.5B invested in private companies and funds plus $3.5B of guarantees for early-stage companies (10-K MD&A); exposure to customers' ability to pay.
3. **Inventory and supply commitments** — inventory up 112% to $21.4B (PR-Q4) and provisions of $7.2B (10-K MD&A); a demand pause would show up here first.

## Three questions management should answer
1. How much FY26 revenue came from companies NVIDIA has invested in or guaranteed, and how is that credit risk monitored?
2. What gross margin should the Board expect through the Rubin transition, given the Hopper → Blackwell transition cost ~4 points in FY26?
3. If China Data Center compute stays at zero, what replaces it — and how dependent is the $78.0B Q1 guide on the two largest customers?

---
*Note: earnings quality — about $9B of FY26 other income was unrealised equity gains (10-K MD&A), and the effective tax rate rises to 17–19% in FY27 (PR-Q4).*
