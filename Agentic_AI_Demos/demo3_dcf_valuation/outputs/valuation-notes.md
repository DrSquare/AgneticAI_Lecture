**What drives the base case**
- Revenue grows from $403B (FY2025) to ~$823B (2030E); Google Cloud rises from 15% to ~35% of revenue (68% growth in 2026E, slowing to 20% by 2030E; backlog $514B).
- Capex ≈ $199B in 2026E (guidance $195–205B) and ~$262B in 2027E, so unlevered FCF is negative in 2026–27 and recovers to ~$151B by 2030E.
- WACC 9.5% (CAPM: 4.2% risk-free + 1.21 beta × 4.5% ERP); terminal growth 3.0%; terminal capex = 1.1× D&A. Terminal value is ~95% of EV.

**Read-across:** the DCF sits below the market price. To justify ~$341 the DCF needs roughly WACC 8.0% with 3.5% terminal growth, or 8.5% with 4.0% (Sensitivity tab), or faster Cloud growth, higher long-run margins, or a quicker return to normal capex. Non-marketable equity stakes (a $99B gain in Q2 2026) are not valued separately and are potential upside.
